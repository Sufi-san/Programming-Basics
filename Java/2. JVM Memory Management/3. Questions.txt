Revision Questions (Capture the Crux):

    Core Components: List the main memory areas within the JVM (JDK 11 onwards) and briefly describe what each is primarily used for.

    Stack vs. Heap:

        What kind of data is stored in the Stack vs. the Heap?
        How is memory managed in each?
        What are the performance characteristics and limitations of each (e.g., speed, size, error types)?
        How do objects (reference types) and primitives interact with Stack and Heap memory?

    Garbage Collection (GC) Fundamentals:

        What is the primary purpose of JVM's Garbage Collection?
        What are "GC Roots," and why are they important in GC?
        Explain the two (or three) main phases of a Mark & Sweep GC.
        What are the three main goals or metrics used to evaluate GC performance?

    Generational GC:

        Explain the concept of "generational" garbage collection in JVM.
        Describe the difference between "Young Generation" and "Old Generation" in the Heap.

    Minor vs. Major GC:

        When is a Minor GC typically triggered, and what memory areas does it primarily clean?
        When is a Major GC typically triggered, and what memory areas does it clean?
        What is "premature promotion" during Minor GC?

    GC Collectors (JDK 11+):

        Name at least three different GC algorithms available in JDK 11 and state their primary focus (e.g., throughput, low pause).
        Which GC is often the default on modern machines with large memory?
        Which experimental GC in JDK 11 aims for extremely low latency for very large heaps?

    Memory Tuning: What JVM flags can be used to control the initial and maximum size of the Heap, and the stack size per thread?

    Metaspace: What is Metaspace, and why is it important to consider its size and potential for growth?
